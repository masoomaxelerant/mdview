# FIFA World Cup 2026 — Full Schedule (IST) + Where to Watch in India

**Tournament:** June 11 – July 19, 2026 · Hosted by USA, Canada & Mexico · 48 teams · 104 matches

> All times below are **Indian Standard Time (IST)**. Because the matches are in North America, IST = US Eastern Time + 9 hrs 30 min, so kickoffs in India fall roughly between **9:30 PM and 9:30 AM**. The IST date shown already accounts for the day rollover (most evening US matches play in the early hours of the next day in India).
